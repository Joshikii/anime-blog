The anime industry is booming globally, generating billions in revenue. Yet, the people drawing the frames often earn less than minimum wage.

The Production Committee Problem

The system is designed to minimize risk for investors but maximizes the burden on studios.

Tight Deadlines: Episodes are often finished hours before airing.

Outsourcing: Relying heavily on freelancers breaks continuity.

If we want anime to survive the next decade, structural reform is not just an option; it's a necessity.
