When Fujimoto penned Chainsaw Man, few expected it to become the cultural phenomenon it is today. The anime adaptation by Studio MAPPA brings this chaotic energy to life with stunning fluidity.

A Break from Tropes

Denji is not your typical Shonen protagonist. He doesn't want to become the Hokage or the Pirate King. He just wants toast with jam and a soft bed.

The Animation: The use of CGI is controversial but effective here.

The Soundtrack: Kensuke Ushio delivers a jagged, industrial score.

It's a story about dreams, however small they may be, and the price we pay to achieve them in a world that wants to consume us.
