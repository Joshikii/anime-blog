The wait is almost over. Solo Leveling will return for a second cour, titled "Arise from the Shadow".

What to Expect

After the intense finale of season 1, Jin-Woo is now the Shadow Monarch. The stakes are higher, and the dungeons are deadlier.

New Hunters: We will finally meet the National Level Hunters.

Better Pacing: The director promised to expand on the manhwa's lore.

Stay tuned for more updates as we approach the winter release window.
