Neon Genesis Evangelion is not really about robots fighting aliens. It's a therapy session written large across the skyline of Tokyo-3.

"I Mustn't Run Away"

Shinji's mantra is famous, but often misunderstood. It's not a battle cry; it's a desperate plea for connection in a world that hurts him.

The genius of Anno was to put a real, flawed, scared teenager inside a giant weapon of mass destruction and ask: How would you really feel?
